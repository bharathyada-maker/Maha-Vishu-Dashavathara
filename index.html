PROLOGUE — SREE MAHA VISHU
ETERNAL ORIGIN
Symbol: Sheshanaga & The Cosmic Ocean (ఆదిశేషుడు / क्षीर सागर)
Before the dawn of creation, Sree Maha Vishu rested upon Ananta in eternal contemplation. Whenever dharma fades and darkness rises, Sree Maha Vishu descends.
शान्ताकारं भुजगशयनं पद्मनाभं सुरेशं विश्वाधारं गगनसदृशं मेघवर्णं शुभाङ्गम् ।
"Salutations to Sree Maha Vishu, embodiment of supreme peace, who rests upon the serpent Ananta, the foundation of all existence."
